Attacks

Roll single or multiple attacks against one or more targets.




This tool rolls attacks by a group of creatures of the same type (or with the same THAC0, number of attacks and damage dice), against one or more targets.

Enter damage dice for all attacks per round, such as "d8+1" or "d4/d4/d6".

Enter ACs for all targets as a comma-separated list, indicating targets with multiple attackers as ACx#. For example, if a target with AC0 is being attacked by 2 creatures, enter it as "0x2". Example: A party is attacked by five ghouls. PCs with AC 3 and 0 are attacked by two ghouls each, The remaining one attacks another PC with AC0. The AC list would be "3x2, 0x2, 0". Spaces are optional.

Clicking Roll performs all attacks and reports hits and damage for all targets.
